What good tutoring looks like

Our shared teaching standard.

Notice

Watch the student’s method, not only the final answer.

Question

Use prompts that reveal how the learner is thinking.

Model

Demonstrate just enough, then return agency to the student.

Record

Capture a useful next step for class and home practice.
