Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

1. Information we use

Contact, registration, and profile details help the centre respond to enquiries, place students in suitable batches, and provide account support.

2. Student information

Student details should be limited to information relevant to classes, communication, attendance, materials, and academic support.

3. Display preferences

The website uses browser storage to remember light or dark mode and left-to-right or right-to-left direction preferences. These can be cleared in browser settings.

4. Map content

The contact page embeds an OpenStreetMap view. Loading the map may send a request to the map provider under its own privacy terms.

5. Your choices

You may ask the centre to review or correct contact and profile details associated with your student account.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, use the details shown on the contact page .
