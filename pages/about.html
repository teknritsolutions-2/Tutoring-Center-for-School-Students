About Fieldnote

Strong routines, small batches, and honest communication build steady academic confidence.

Why Fieldnote began

Students needed a clearer way to see their own progress.

Fieldnote was created around one simple idea: tutoring works best when each class leaves a useful trace—an insight, a correction, or a next step.

Our principles

What students can expect every week.

01

Clarity first

Understand why a method works before building speed and independence.

02

Questions welcome

Use confusion as useful information and ask before a gap grows.

03

Useful feedback

04

Visible progress

Report real gains clearly while keeping the next priority visible.

How the centre works
