Course overview

Move from “I followed it” to “I can solve it.”

This pathway helps students connect methods, choose strategies, explain steps, and recover from common errors.

Learning levels

Four stages through the term.

01

Secure the base

Number properties, algebraic manipulation, linear equations, and essential geometry.

02

Connect representations

Move confidently between equations, graphs, tables, diagrams, and written reasoning.

03

Build mixed-strategy fluency

Identify which method a problem needs and compare efficient solution paths.

04

Practise under exam conditions

Plan time, show complete working, check efficiently, and review error patterns.
